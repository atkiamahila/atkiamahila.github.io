Atkia Mahila

Ph.D. Student, Computing and Information Sciences
Rochester Institute of Technology

I am a Ph.D. student in Computing and Information Sciences at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. M. Mustafa Rafique in the HPDSL lab. I work where high-performance computing meets natural language processing: my research asks how large language models should spend compute when they reason at inference time, and how the systems that run them determine what that reasoning actually costs.

Much of my work centers on tree-structured reasoning, where a model explores many partial solutions in parallel and a verifier decides which branches to keep. These workloads stress serving systems in unusual ways: branches share long prefixes, KV cache memory fills quickly, and scoring and scheduling can dominate wall-clock time. I study both sides of the problem, the search algorithm and the runtime underneath it.

Interests: test-time compute and reasoning search, efficient LLM inference and serving, KV cache management, and performance characterization of AI workloads on parallel and HPC systems.
